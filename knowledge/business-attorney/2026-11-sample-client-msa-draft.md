## Master Services Agreement (draft received from the client)

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **Example Law Group, P.A.**, a 12-attorney personal-injury firm in Palm Beach County ("Client"), and **Dautel Intelligent Operations, LLC** ("Consultant").

**1. Services.** Consultant shall provide all services necessary to automate Client's intake process, including any additional services Client reasonably requests during the Term, for the fee in Section 2.

**2. Fees and payment.** Client shall pay a fixed fee of **$9,000**. No deposit is due. Payment is due **ninety (90) days after Client's written acceptance**, which Client may give or withhold in its sole discretion.

**3. Intellectual property.** All work product, including Consultant's **pre-existing tools, templates, code libraries, prompts, methods and know-how** used in performing the Services, is assigned to Client upon creation.

**4. Warranty.** Consultant warrants that all outputs of the intake system, including AI-generated summaries and messages to prospective clients, **will be accurate and error-free for twelve (12) months** after go-live.

**5. Limitation of liability.** Client's total liability under this Agreement is limited to **$1,000**. **Consultant's liability is not limited.**

**6. Indemnification.** Consultant shall defend and indemnify Client against **any claim arising from the use of the intake system**, including claims of legal malpractice, missed limitation deadlines and unauthorized practice of law.

**7. Insurance.** Consultant shall carry professional liability (E&O) insurance of **$5,000,000** and cyber liability insurance of **$5,000,000**, naming Client as additional insured.

**8. Data.** Consultant may store Client data, including **privileged attorney-client communications and prospective-client medical records**, on Consultant's own servers for performance and training purposes.

**9. Support.** Consultant shall provide **24/7 support with a one-hour response time** at no additional charge for the Term.

**10. Non-competition.** For **three (3) years** after the Term, Consultant shall not provide services to **any law firm in Palm Beach County**.

**11. Termination.** Client may terminate this Agreement **for convenience at any time, with no payment due for work in progress**.

**12. Governing law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of New York**, with exclusive venue in **New York County**.

**13. Term.** Two (2) years, renewing automatically for successive two-year terms unless Client gives notice.
