# Research: an AI consulting and deployment firm in Boynton Beach, Florida

This folder is the shared library for the expert agents in [`.claude/agents/`](../.claude/agents/). It describes **Dautel Intelligent Operations (DIO)**, an AI consulting and deployment firm founded by J. Erich Dautel in Boynton Beach, Palm Beach County: the market, the customers, the competition, the costs, the permits, the team, the marketing and the biggest risks. Every agent reads these files before it advises.

**Where it came from:** most of it is the November 2026 business plan research from the DIO operating manual (gen-skeptic/experts), split here into one file per topic. New research done on November 25, 2026 fills the gaps the topics asked for: Boynton Beach and Palm Beach County permits and business tax, opening and monthly costs, the local AI competitors, Boynton Beach market figures and the status of Florida's 2026 AI bills. Each file says which parts are new.

**How to read the figures:** third-party numbers are point-in-time and cited with dates; estimates are labeled *(est.)*. Recheck anything before it goes into a pitch, a contract or a filing.
