## 11. Team plan and salary ranges

DIO starts as a one-person company with outside advisors. Erich is the owner and **Chairman**; the CEO agent, `orchestrator`, runs day-to-day operations, delegates the work to the other agents and reports to him monthly (P46). The reporting lines are in [expert-roster.md](expert-roster.md). Each later role is added when revenue can pay for it, not on a calendar date. Every role below has a matching agent in `.claude/agents/` (named in the last column). The agents can cover much of a role's drafting and research before a person is hired, but a human reviews everything they produce.

**How to read the ranges:** US base pay in 2026 for a small firm hiring in South Florida or remote US, at mid-to-senior level. Florida has no state income tax, which helps when competing with high-tax metros. Ranges marked *(est.)* are planning estimates; verify them against current postings (Built In, Robert Half, Glassdoor, Indeed) before making an offer. Contractor and fractional rates are what DIO would *pay*, not what it bills.

### 11.7 Fully loaded cost and hiring math

- **Loaded cost** = base × **1.25–1.40** (payroll taxes, health benefits, 401(k) match, insurance, equipment, software, training). A $150K engineer costs about $190K–$210K a year.
- **Hiring trigger** (from [§10](06-startup-and-operating-costs.md)): sustained revenue the new person will deliver or protect ≥ **1.5 × loaded cost**, plus three months of their loaded cost in cash reserve.
- **Billable engineer check:** about 1,200–1,400 billable hours a year at a blended $175–$225/hr gives $210K–$315K of capacity. Hire only when backlog and retainers will fill at least 70% of it.
- **Founder pay:** take an owner's draw until the CPA recommends an S-corp election, then a reasonable salary plus distributions.

### 11.9 Hiring priorities

1. **Contract senior agent engineer:** more build capacity, the fastest revenue lever.
2. **Part-time content marketer:** a steadier pipeline.
3. **Full-time platform/DevOps engineer:** retainer revenue that scales without the founder.
4. **Client success manager:** retention and expansion.

**Sourcing channels:** KubeCraft and CNCF communities, FAU graduates and MBA network, veteran transition programs (SkillBridge, Hiring Our Heroes), referrals from partner MSPs, and contractors who did good work on earlier projects.
