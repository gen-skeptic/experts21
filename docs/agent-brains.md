# Agent brains and personalities

*Generated from `tools/team.py` and `tools/brains.py` by `tools/build.py`.*

Every agent has a **brain** (the model it runs on and how hard it thinks), a **personality** (a Star Trek name, a voice and a working style) and a **knowledge folder** (`knowledge/<agent>/`) it reads before it answers.

## The brains

| Brain | Model (`model:` alias) | Strengths | Price per million tokens (input / output) | Speed | Agents |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | -: |
| **Fast** | Claude Haiku 5.5 (`haiku`) | Very fast and inexpensive for high-volume, well-defined tasks | $0.10 / $0.50 (prompts up to 100K tokens; $0.50 / $2.50 beyond) | Fastest. | 4 |
| **Standard** | Claude Sonnet 5.5 (`sonnet`) | Fast, capable everyday work: coding, agents, writing, analysis, planning | $2 / $10 | Fast. | 34 |
| **Deep** | Claude Opus 5.5 (`opus`) | Careful judgment on complex, ambiguous or high-stakes work | $4 / $20 | Moderate. | 12 |
| **Frontier** | Claude Fable 5.2 (`fable`) | Anthropic's most capable widely released model: the hardest reasoning and long-horizon agentic work | $8 / $40 | Slowest. A hard task can take many minutes in a single turn. | 0 |

All four brains have a 1M-token context window and up to 128K tokens of output. Costs are list prices per million tokens (input / output) on the Claude API in November 2026; subscription plans meter usage differently, but the ratios still show the relative cost.

**How the defaults were chosen:** **Opus** (Deep) for judgment where a mistake is expensive: strategy, legal, finance, risk, architecture, security, statistics and the QA gate. **Sonnet** (Standard) for most specialist work: building, research, writing, planning and coordination. **Haiku** (Fast) for fast, simple, high-volume work: scheduling and admin, short-form posts, clips and community replies. Frontier (`fable`) is never a default; only the orchestrator assigns it, task by task.

**Totals:** 12 agents on Opus, 34 on Sonnet, 4 on Haiku.

## When to use each brain

| Brain | Use for | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fast** | Short-form copy variants, tagging, summaries, filing records, CRM hygiene, triage, formatting, checklists. | Judgment calls, anything new or ambiguous, and anything client-final without a review by a stronger brain. |
| **Standard** | Building software and automations, research, client documents, content, planning and coordination. | Final calls on money, security, legal or architecture (escalate to Deep), and bulk short-form work (Fast is enough). |
| **Deep** | Strategy, architecture, legal, security and compliance review, QA gates, root-cause analysis, money and risk. | Routine, templated or high-volume work, where it costs more and adds little. |
| **Frontier** | Novel, high-stakes problems the Deep brain has not settled: a new platform architecture, an incident whose cause two passes could not find, a strategic decision with many interacting unknowns. | Anything the Deep brain handles. Never a default; only `orchestrator` assigns it, task by task. |

## Brain by type of work

The orchestrator picks the brain for each assignment from this table: start from the task type, raise one level (effort first, then brain) for money, security, legal terms, client-final work or after a failed review, and lower one level for routine work that a review will catch.

| Task type | Examples | Brain | Effort | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy and high-stakes decisions | Decision memos, quarterly goals, the monthly report to the Chairman, offer and pricing design | Deep (`opus`) | high | Wrong calls are expensive and slow to undo; the work is weighing trade-offs and second-order effects. |
| Architecture and technical design | Solution designs, architecture decision records, platform and data architecture, build-versus-buy | Deep (`opus`) | high | Design mistakes multiply through every later build step. |
| Security, legal and compliance review | Threat models, agent safety audits, contract review, risk reviews | Deep (`opus`) | high | A missed issue costs more than the review; needs slow, careful reading. |
| QA gates and fact-checks | Every QA step, estimate and pricing checks, claims in published content | Deep (`opus`) | high | A gate is only as good as its reviewer; a weak review passes defects downstream. Use medium for routine internal items such as social posts. |
| Root-cause analysis and statistics | Incident reviews, DMAIC analysis, control charts, measurement system checks | Deep (`opus`) | high | Correct causes and correct statistics need deliberate reasoning. |
| Software and automation build | Features, APIs, agent workflows, data pipelines, tests, infrastructure as code | Standard (`sonnet`) | medium | Everyday coding and multi-step tool use; escalate to Deep for security-sensitive code, concurrency or a hard bug. |
| Infrastructure and production changes | Deployment plans, migrations, runbooks, rollback plans | Standard (`sonnet`) | high | Production work needs care; QA by the Deep brain is the gate before release. |
| Research and analysis | Market research, competitor matrices, data analysis, financial models, vertical briefs | Standard (`sonnet`) | high | Thoroughness matters more than speed; sources must be read, not skimmed. |
| Client-facing documents | Proposals, SOWs, assessment reports, QBR decks, case studies | Standard (`sonnet`) | high | Clients read every word; money and claims inside still go through a Deep QA gate. |
| Long-form content and training | Articles, newsletters, scripts, curricula, workshop materials | Standard (`sonnet`) | medium | Good writing at a sensible cost; fact-checks happen at the QA step. |
| Planning, coordination and communication | Routing plans, assignments, schedules, status reports, check-ins, support replies, outreach drafts | Standard (`sonnet`) | medium | Clear, reliable everyday work. |
| Short-form and high-volume content | Social posts, captions, hooks, ad variants, titles and meta descriptions, thumbnail text | Fast (`haiku`) | medium | Many small, well-defined pieces; review catches the rare miss. |
| Routine operations | Filing records, CRM hygiene, tagging, summaries of long threads, formatting, reminders, monitoring triage | Fast (`haiku`) | low | Templated steps where speed and cost matter most. |
| Hardest problems (CEO escalation only) | A problem the Deep brain has not settled after two passes, or a novel decision with many interacting unknowns | Frontier (`fable`) | high | Most capable brain, at about twice the Deep brain's cost and with much longer turns; only `orchestrator` assigns it. |
