### 3.4 Competitive landscape

| Competitor type | Strengths | Weaknesses DIO exploits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No-code AI automation agencies** (Zapier/Make/n8n builders, chatbot shops) | Cheap, fast, lots of marketing | No security model, no identity or credential scoping, brittle, no recovery, vendor lock-in |
| **Freelance marketplaces** (Upwork, Fiverr) | Lowest price | No accountability, no operating ownership |
| **MSPs / IT providers** | Trusted by SMBs, 24/7 desks | Thin agent and MCP expertise; mostly resell Copilot licences. *Potential partners* ([§7.3](09-marketing.md)) |
| **Vertical SaaS with embedded agents** | Turnkey | Silos; nobody connects them to each other or to company memory |
| **Big consultancies** | Brand | Priced out of the SMB market ($400–$1,000+/hr) |
| **UVM tech vendors** (AiDash, Sheltera, GE Vernova, Optelos) | Satellite, LiDAR, AI products | Sell to utilities top-down; weak on contractor and field-crew operations integration. *Future partner or target* ([§4](05-services-and-pricing.md), Line E) |

**DIO's differentiation in one line:** *the only local practice that will build your agents, run them on infrastructure you control, and hand you runbooks showing how to review, verify and recover them.*

| **Local managed IT providers** (seven found within 10 miles) | Trusted IT support for the same firms ([local prospects](local-prospects.md), §7) | Partners first: refer agent and process work to us, and we refer desk support to them |

**Takeaways**

1. The local market is **new and crowded at the low end**: most of these firms started in 2025 or 2026 and lead with automation, not governance.
2. **Nobody in the scan leads with "fix the process first"** or with Lean Six Sigma. That stays the positioning.
3. **Prices are not published** by most local firms. SMB Advantage's $5,000 minimum sits inside our assessment-to-build range, so our published starting prices ($1,500 audit, $2,500 assessment) give buyers a cheaper first step.
4. Directory ratings are easy to buy. Our proof should be things a buyer can check: case studies with client permission, public GitHub templates and recorded demos.
