# 90-day plan and open questions

*Source: copied from the DIO business plan research in gen-skeptic/experts (`research/dautel-intelligent-operations-business-plan-research.md`, compiled November 19, 2026), updated November 25, 2026 where noted. Process numbers such as P33 refer to the DIO operating manual in that repository; in this repository the same methods are skills in `.claude/skills/`.*

The first 90 days, and the questions only the founder can answer.

## 14. 90-day action plan

**Days 1–30: foundation**
1. Review the employer policy and document clearance.
2. Form the LLC, EIN and bank account; get insurance quotes.
3. Draft the services page, pricing and booking link on dautel.io; move the resume to /about.
4. Write the Readiness Assessment playbook (interview guide, scoring rubric, report template) and the Process Health Check playbook (SIPOC, value stream map, baseline, waste walk, readiness verdict).

**Days 31–60: first clients**
5. Recruit 2–3 **founding clients** (FAU network, personal network, a local field-service or professional-services firm) at about 40% off in exchange for a case study.
6. Meet 3 local MSPs and IT providers to propose a referral partnership.
7. Publish the "Agent Safety Checklist" lead magnet; start the LinkedIn cadence.
8. Add the remote offers: list the Agent Safety Audit and MCP Integration Sprint on Upwork/Contra, add Stripe payment links and a "How remote engagements work" page ([§8](09-marketing.md)).

**Days 61–90: proof and pipeline**
9. Deliver the first assessment and the first governed workflow build.
10. Give the first free workshop (chamber or SBDC).
11. Publish the first client case study and open-source the MCP server template; convert at least one client to a retainer.
12. Revisit pricing and ICP based on real conversations; update this plan.

## 15. Open questions for the founder

1. What does the NextEra outside-employment policy allow? This shapes Line E's timing.
2. How many hours a week can go to DIO through MBA completion?
3. Is healthcare (HIPAA) in scope for year 1, or deferred?
4. Is the recommended local/remote mix (70/30 shifting to 30/70, [§8.7](09-marketing.md)) right, or go remote-national from day one?
5. Is a company-memory **product** a long-term ambition (SaaS), or does memory stay an internal accelerator and a services line? (A product can't be built on Open Brain or OpsBrain under their license.)
6. Personal income target that defines "full-time ready"?
7. ~~Where should task cards, decision records and QA records live (the system of record)?~~ **Decided: OpsBrain**, DIO's self-hosted system of record ([§9](10-operations-and-delivery.md)).
8. Who is the backup approver when Erich is unavailable?
9. What warranty period goes into SOWs (proposed default: 30 days after acceptance)?
10. What support hours and response targets go with each retainer tier?
