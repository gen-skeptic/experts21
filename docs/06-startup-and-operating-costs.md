### Opening costs in Boynton Beach (checked November 25, 2026)

| Item | Cost | Source and notes |

### Monthly operating costs (estimates)

| Item | Home-based | With a coworking membership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Insurance (bundled tech E&O and cyber, about $1,200–$3,000 a year) | $100–$250 | $100–$250 |
| Model APIs, VPS, scheduling, accounting and other tools | $150–$300 | $150–$300 |
| Workspace | $0 (home office) | $249–$399 at CoWork Boynton (membership or dedicated desk); private offices nearby from about $600 ([CommercialSearch](https://www.commercialsearch.com/coworking-property/us/fl/boynton-beach/cowork-boynton), [CoworkingCafe](https://www.coworkingcafe.com/office-space/us/fl/boynton-beach/)) |
| Meeting rooms for client workshops | Day passes from $20 and rooms from $24 an hour at CoWork Boynton; Regus day coworking from $45 ([Regus](https://www.regus.com/en/us/florida/boynton-beach/coworking)) | Often included |
| Marketing (events, small ads, collateral; $1,000–$2,000 a year) | $85–$170 | $85–$170 |
| **Total** | **About $335–$720 a month** | **About $585–$1,120 a month** |

Prices from listing sites can be out of date. Call the operators and brokers before committing.

**Recommendation:** start home-based (with the city's home-based business tax receipt), meet clients at their offices or book a meeting room for workshops, and add a coworking membership only when client meetings become weekly.


## 10. Financial outline (illustrative, to be refined)

### Revenue scenarios
| | 2027 (side practice) | 2028 (transition) | 2029 (full-time) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Assessments | 6 × $3K = $18K | 12 × $4K = $48K | 18 × $5K = $90K |
| Builds | 3 × $9K = $27K | 8 × $12K = $96K | 12 × $15K = $180K |
| Retainers (avg MRR at year end) | 3 × $1.2K → ~$20K/yr | 8 × $1.8K → ~$110K/yr | 14 × $2.2K → ~$300K/yr |
| Workshops | $5K | $15K | $25K |
| **Approx. revenue** | **~$70K** | **~$270K** | **~$595K** |

**Process improvement (Line H) is upside not included above.** Four to six Process Health Checks and one or two Kaizen events in 2027 add roughly $20K–$50K, and they feed the build pipeline.

**Custom software (Line G) is upside not included above.** Two or three internal-tool or MVP builds a year at about $25K each adds roughly $50K–$75K in 2028, plus Software Care revenue. Treat it as upside until the software team exists.

**Full-time transition trigger (suggested):** recurring retainer revenue ≥ **$8–10K/mo** for three consecutive months, **plus** six months of personal expenses in reserve, **plus** a pipeline of ≥ two signed builds. Timing aligns with MBA completion (2027).

**Scaling path after 2029:** one subcontract engineer for builds, an MSP partnership for desk coverage, possible "company memory" product for SMBs, built independently of Open Brain and OpsBrain (license, [§13](11-risks.md)).
